# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Calibrax to be a respectful, practical, and technically rigorous
project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to participate in a way that
is constructive and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Use respectful, precise language.
- Focus criticism on code, design, data, and tradeoffs.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them promptly.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Sustained disruption of technical discussion.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or limit participation when
behavior violates this code. Serious or repeated violations can result in a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to security@avitai.bio.
